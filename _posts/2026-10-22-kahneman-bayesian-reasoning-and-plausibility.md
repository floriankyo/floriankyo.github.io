## How to Discipline Intuition

> The essential keys to disciplined Bayesian reasoning can be simply summarized:
>
> - Anchor your judgment of the probability of an outcome on a plausible base rate.
> - Question the diagnosticity of your evidence.

## Linda: Less is More

> The most coherent stories are not necessarily the most probable, but they are *plausible*, and the notions of coherence, plausibility, and probability are easily confused by the unwary.
>
> …
>
> As expected, probability judgments were higher for the richer and more detailed scenario, contrary to logic. This is a trap for forecasters and their clients: adding detail to scenarios makes them more persuasive, but less likely to come true.

Source: Daniel Kahneman, *Thinking, Fast and Slow* (2011)

## 如何约束直觉

> 严谨的贝叶斯推理，其要点可以简单概括为：
>
> - 以一个合理的基础比率为锚点，来判断某个结果的概率。
> - 质疑你所掌握证据的诊断价值。

## 琳达：少即是多

> 最连贯的故事未必是最可能发生的，但它们*看似合理*；而连贯性、合理性与可能性这几个概念，很容易被粗心的人混为一谈。
>
> ……
>
> 不出所料，人们对内容更丰富、细节更多的情境给出了更高的概率判断，这与逻辑相悖。这是预测者及其客户容易掉进的陷阱：为情境添加细节会让它更有说服力，却也让它更不可能成真。

出处：丹尼尔·卡尼曼，《思考，快与慢》（2011）。中文为笔者自译。
