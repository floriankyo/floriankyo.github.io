## After The Crescent Moon

I have finished Tagore's *The Crescent Moon*. It is a classic, and for me as a modern reader it was not always easy: the world in those pages feels far from the one I live in. Still, I finished it. The writing is simple. I would not say it is much better than many modern books. To me it is a perspective — a window onto how people thought about a hundred years ago. That kind of thinking has not changed as much as we might assume. And children remain what they have always been: among the best things humankind has, and among the most important.

## Publishing more

Quantity matters more to me right now than polish. Publishing regularly forces practice. With AI in the mix, I would rather embrace abundance than wait for perfect posts.

Looking ahead, I want this website to hold more: more kinds of writing, and more often. The blog is becoming an archive of everything I put out across the net — a place where the work accumulates.

## 读完《新月集》之后

我已经读完泰戈尔的《新月集》。它是一部经典；对我这样一个当代读者来说，并不总是轻松——书里的世界与我所生活的时代相去甚远。但我还是读完了。文字很朴素。我并不认为它比许多当代书籍高出许多。对我而言，它更像一种视角：一扇窗，让人看见大约一百年前人们如何思考。而这种思考方式，或许并未像我们想象的那样剧烈改变。孩子依然是人类最宝贵、也最为重要的事物之一。

## 多发表一些

此刻对我来说，数量比打磨更为重要。持续发表迫使练习。有了人工智能，我宁愿拥抱丰富，也不愿一味等待完美的文章。

往后，我希望这个网站容纳更多：更多种类的写作，也更频繁地发表。博客逐渐成为我在网络上发表过的一切内容的档案——作品在这里不断积累。
